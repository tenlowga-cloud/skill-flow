# The phone step: install the app, allow notifications

A routine that runs at 7:30 is useless if the owner sees it at noon. The phone is how the morning brief, the meeting note and the GoHighLevel sweep reach them where they are. Do this step after the first routine is scheduled, in teaching mode, one step per message.

## 1. Install the app

Ask which phone. Then:

- **iPhone:** "Open the App Store. Search for Claude. The maker is Anthropic. Tap Get. You should see Claude on your home screen when it finishes."
- **Android:** "Open the Play Store. Search for Claude. The maker is Anthropic. Tap Install. You should see Claude in your apps when it finishes."

If their AI is not Claude (ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok), name that app instead; the steps are the same shape.

## 2. Sign in with the same account

"Open the app and sign in with the same email you use on your computer. You should see your recent chats from today in the list. If you do not, you signed in with a different account; sign out and try again."

The same account is what makes the profile, the memory and the routines show up on the phone.

## 3. Allow notifications

"The app asks whether it may send notifications. Tap Allow."

If they already tapped Don't Allow, or nothing asked:

- **iPhone:** "Open Settings on your phone, scroll to Claude, tap Notifications, turn Allow Notifications on. Leave Sounds and Badges on."
- **Android:** "Open Settings, Apps, Claude, Notifications, turn them on."

Then inside the app: "Open the app's settings (your profile picture or the menu). Look for Notifications. Turn on everything about tasks, scheduled tasks or routines finishing. The labels vary; read me what you see and we will pick together."

## 4. Test it

Schedule a one-off routine two minutes out ("in 2 minutes, send me the line: phone test passed") on the surface that runs the owner's routines. Wait. "Did your phone buzz with that line?" Yes: record "phone: notifications on, tested" in `STACK.md`. No: go back to step 3, check the phone's Focus or Do Not Disturb mode, and try once more. Still no: record "phone: app installed, notifications unverified" and the one thing to check, and move on; the routines still land in the app.

## 5. Tell them how it will feel

"Tomorrow at [time] your phone will buzz with the morning brief. Open it, read the three lines, tap the questions. That is the whole routine. Everything else is already done when you see it."

## Rules

- Never ask for their phone number. Notifications go through the app, not SMS.
- Never set up notifications for anyone other than the owner.
- If they say no to notifications, record it and stop. Routines still appear in the app when they open it.
