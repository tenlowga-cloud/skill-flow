# Pre-meeting brief template

One page. Under 300 words of body. Fill every section. If you have nothing, write "unknown" or "none found". Do not pad.

## Rules

1. Every fact carries its source in brackets: [calendar], [email 2026-10-12 "Subject"], [CRM deal 4412], [note: filename], [call 2026-09-30].
2. Nothing invented. No guessing a title, a budget, or a mood.
3. Quote their words when you can. Mark who said it and where.
4. Never copy a secret, password, ID number, or account number into the brief. Name the source file instead.
5. Write in VOICE.md style for any wording the owner may reuse (questions, goal). Facts stay plain.

## Template

```
MEETING: <title> | <day, time, place or link> | <length>

WHO
<Name>, <role>, <company>. Came to us via <how> [source].
Others attending: <names, roles> [calendar].

LAST 3 TOUCHES
1. <date>: <what happened, one line> [source]
2. <date>: <...> [source]
3. <date>: <...> [source]

WHAT THEY CARE ABOUT
- "<their words>" [source]
- "<their words>" [source]

OPEN ITEMS
- We promised: <item, date> [source]
- They owe us: <item> [source]
- Unanswered: <question, date> [source]

MONEY
Stage: <stage>. Value: <amount>. Renewal or close date: <date>. [CRM]
(If no CRM: "Not in a connected tool.")

RISKS AND RED FLAGS
- <risk, with evidence> [source]

GOAL FOR THIS MEETING
<One line. What does a good outcome look like?>

3 QUESTIONS TO ASK
1.
2.
3.

DO NOT BRING UP (only if evidence)
<topic, why> [source]

SOURCES
<file names, email ids, CRM record ids, call dates. No secret contents.>
NOT CHECKED: <tools that were missing or failed>
```

## Notes for the writer

- **Goal**: take it from the calendar description or last open item. If unclear, propose one and mark it "proposed".
- **Questions**: tie each to an open item or something they said. No generic questions.
- **Do not bring up**: only when there is a real reason, such as a past complaint, a sensitive event they mentioned, or an unresolved dispute. Skip the section if there is none.
- **Money**: show what the CRM says. Do not do math the CRM did not do.
- **Risks**: late replies, a competitor mentioned, a stalled deal, a past complaint, a changed contact. Always with evidence.
- **Two people with the same name**: see sources.md. State which one you matched and why.
- **Short on time or data**: send what you have, with "NOT CHECKED" filled in. A thin true brief beats a full guess.
- **Delivery**: to the owner only, 60 minutes before. Do not send to the attendee.
