# Creative Direction

Part of [skill-flow](../../README.md). The `flow` skill decides when this runs; `setup` fills the profile files it reads.

Make work with a point of view that could belong only to this project. Minimal, high energy, premium, easy to use.

Read `PROFILE.md` from your skill-flow profile folder (`~/.claude/skill-flow/` in Claude Code, else `profile/` in this repo). If it is missing, say so once and ask the one question you need, or run `setup`. Also read `VOICE.md` for any copy. The owner's newest words and the chosen direction win. Bar: the quality bar in `PROFILE.md` (default: right the first time, complete, proven, no homework for the owner), scored in the proof below.

## Identity lens

The owner may work across many fields. Never default to one field as the audience, subject, metaphor, buyer, look or business model for unrelated work. Find the real field from the task.

## Understand the experience

Keep: the outcome in the owner's words; audience, moment and state of mind; what they should feel, understand, remember and do; exact examples and anti-examples; fixed truth, brand, accessibility and safety; creative latitude; the proof standard.

Look it up before you ask. If a material choice is still the owner's, ask 1 to 4 short questions up front, recommended first. Then run to done.

## Build the reference field

The owner's examples first: open each, record what it gives and what must not transfer. For material new work, widen the evidence before locking a direction: images and brand assets from where `PROFILE.md` says the owner keeps them, live web and category leaders, adjacent and distant fields, image search, real sources. File research in the owner's notes (where `PROFILE.md` says they live) and read it first to reuse earlier sweeps. Record per source: path or link, observed facts, contribution, non-transferable element, connector, rights status. Full recipe: `references/reference-field.md`.

Personal, client, case and ambiguous private images are never inspiration.

## Make the connector

Name the answer this category would produce automatically and the assumption that makes it feel inevitable. Ask what other field solved the same human problem differently.

For net-new or unclear work, build a small set of genuinely different central ideas. Each differs in meaning and grammar, not just palette, font or effect. Recommend the one that best joins recognition, clarity, emotion, action and originality.

No direction round for a small repair or a chosen world: keep the approved idea, fix the weak part. An unexpected link must improve meaning, memory, clarity, feeling or use, or it is random novelty.

## Copy and story

- Make the reader see something: a concrete scene, analogy or contrast. One strong analogy beats a pile of clever lines.
- Truth controls the image. Never invent a fact, person, quote, number, event or pain point.
- Reject copy another company could use unchanged.
- Fewest words that land. Short, direct, no em dashes, in the owner's voice from `VOICE.md` (the `ghost-mode` skill, if the owner has it on).
- Bring in a domain fact-check only when the project belongs to that domain.

## Visual and graphic work

One world owns the artifact: composition, type, image, color, material, copy, motion and interaction carry the same idea. Go minimal: one focal point per screen, generous space, few type sizes and colors, one primary action. Remove before you add. Premium is restraint and craft, not more effects. Reject unexamined defaults (list: `references/reference-field.md`).

## Generated comps

Use them when they reveal a choice before code or production hardens it. Truly different worlds, a role for every input image, outputs saved with prompt and reference sidecar, then inspected. Use the image tool in `TOOLS.md`, and only when the job has the credit and commercial-use rights it needs. Details: `references/reference-field.md`.

## No-slop proof

Before finishing, answer with real artifact evidence:

- What is the central idea?
- Which visible or verbal choice traces to each important reference?
- What cross-domain connector was invented, and why does it serve this audience?
- What familiar category answer was rejected?
- Could a neighboring company swap in its logo and use this unchanged?
- Do copy, type, imagery, composition and interaction belong to one world?
- Was the real artifact checked in its real medium, states, sizes and motion?
- Quality bar scored (one point, less, energy, premium, time back, one tap), each 9 or higher?

Generic is not done, even if technically complete. Repair the idea or execution, then compare again.
