# Draft patterns

These are shapes to fill in, not text to paste. Each is 2 to 5 sentences.

**VOICE.md overrides every pattern here.** Its sample messages, greeting, sign-off, length, and banned words win. If a pattern sounds unlike the samples, change the pattern. If a facts line is missing (price, date, name), leave a clear [bracket] for the owner. Never invent it.

All drafts are saved as drafts. The owner taps send.

---

## 1. Reply to a quote request
- **Use when**: someone asks for a price or estimate.
- **Shape**: Thank them by name, in one short line. Restate what they asked for so they know you got it. Give the price or the next step to get one (visit, call, form). Give a date for when they will hear back or when the quote is good until.
- **Never say**: "best price", "no obligation", or any number the owner did not give you.

## 2. Reply to a complaint
- **Use when**: someone is unhappy. Flag this to the owner first and do not make it a quick reply.
- **Shape**: Start with what went wrong, in their terms. Say sorry once, plainly, if the owner agrees it is their fault; if unsure, say "I hear you" and ask for details. Say what the owner will do and by when. Offer a call.
- **Never say**: "as per our policy", "unfortunately", or anything that blames the customer. Do not promise a refund or fix the owner has not approved.

## 3. Nudge a quiet lead
- **Use when**: no reply after the follow-up window.
- **Shape**: Short opener that references the last thing said. One easy question they can answer in a word. Offer one clear next step. Leave the door open, no guilt.
- **Never say**: "just checking in", "bumping this", "per my last email".

## 4. Confirm a meeting
- **Use when**: a meeting is booked, or the day before.
- **Shape**: Day, time, place or link, in one line. What the owner will cover or what to bring. How to reach the owner if something changes.
- **Never say**: anything not on the calendar. Check the time zone against the invite.

## 5. Send a recap after a meeting
- **Use when**: within a day of a meeting. Facts come from the notes or transcript.
- **Shape**: Thank them in one line. What was decided, in 2 or 3 bullets. Who does what, by when (owner's items and theirs). The next meeting or step.
- **Never say**: anything that was not said in the meeting. Do not add promises or prices.

## 6. Decline politely
- **Use when**: the owner says no to a request, an offer, or a meeting.
- **Shape**: Thank them. Say no clearly in the second sentence, with no long reason. Offer a small alternative only if the owner wants to (another time, a referral). Wish them well.
- **Never say**: a fake reason, "maybe later" if it is a real no, or "I wish I could".

## 7. Introduce two people
- **Use when**: the owner wants to connect two contacts. Check each side agreed first; if not, draft a short permission ask to each.
- **Shape**: Name both, one line on who each is. One line on why they should talk. Hand off: "I'll let you two take it from here." Both on the same message.
- **Never say**: private details about either person, or how much the owner paid or earned from either.

## 8. Ask for a review or referral
- **Use when**: a job just went well or the customer thanked the owner. Not during a complaint or open issue.
- **Shape**: Thank them for something specific. Ask for one thing: a review (with the link from PROFILE.md) or a referral (name one type of person). Say it takes a minute. No pressure.
- **Never say**: "5 stars", offer a reward for a review (breaks many platform rules), or ask twice in one message.

## Checks before saving any draft

1. Read it against two VOICE.md samples. Does it sound like the same person?
2. Banned words from VOICE.md: none present.
3. Names, dates, amounts: each from a source. Gaps in [brackets].
4. Send window: if the owner might send right away, note any time limits from PROFILE.md.
5. Subject line short and clear. Keep the thread's subject on replies.
6. Under 120 words unless the owner's samples run longer.
