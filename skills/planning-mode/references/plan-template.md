# Plan Template

Adapt this structure to the project. Keep every section that carries needed context and mark sections that do not apply. Use clear adult language with enough depth, examples, tradeoffs, ownership and proof for someone to execute the plan without losing the idea.

Planner and writers: in skill-flow, the `planner` agent drafts the plan and `writer` agents execute its slices. A `reviewer` checks the result. See the `flow` skill. Name the planner and each writer in "Who We Need" below, and keep each writer's slice separate from the others.

---

# [Project Name] - Execution Plan

## The Goal
One or two sentences: what we are building and what done looks like.

## Why
What is driving this: deadline, money, a problem on fire.

## What We Already Have
Tools, accounts, data and prior work, confirmed or found in the sources.

## Constraints
Hard limits that cannot change: budget, time, stack, rules.

## The Build (Step by Step)
Numbered steps in order, one clear action each.
1. ...
2. ...
3. ...

## Who We Need (Specialists)
One row per helper. A small task may need only an owner. Read the lanes in `PROFILE.md` for who owns what.

| Type | Specialty | What They Own (Job Description) | When |
|------|-----------|--------------------------------|------|
| AI agent | e.g. workflow automation builder | Exactly what this agent does on this project, in a few short lines. | Phase 1 |
| Outside human | e.g. licensed subject-matter expert | Same level of detail. | Before launch |

## What Could Break It
Failure modes and what to do when each hits.

## Who Runs It After
Who operates it once built, and whether an average person can.

## Assumptions
Each material assumption and how to reverse it. Leave marked blanks for facts we could not verify.

## Open Questions
Only what the owner must answer, as short options with your pick first and marked "(Recommended)". Empty if none.

## Next Move
Build now, or hold as reference.
