# AI tells to remove

This is the general list. The owner's personal banned words and phrases live in `VOICE.md`. Apply both.

## Banned words and phrases
leverage, unlock, elevate, empower, seamless, robust, delve, realm, landscape, tapestry, testament, game-changer, cutting-edge, world-class, best-in-class, revolutionize, supercharge, harness, foster, embark, boast, vibrant, bustling, crucial, vital, pivotal, comprehensive, holistic, streamline, moreover, furthermore, additionally, notably, arguably, ultimately, "navigate" used as a metaphor, "rest assured".

## Banned patterns
- "It's not just X, it's Y", "not only... but also", "more than just". Say the thing once.
- Openers: "In today's world" and every variant, "When it comes to", "It's worth noting", "It's important to note", "Let's dive in", "Picture this".
- Closers: "In conclusion", "At the end of the day", "All in all", "The bottom line is".
- "Did you know..." questions and stacked rhetorical questions.
- Both-sides hedging ("while X, it's also worth considering Y"). Take the side the source supports.
- Neutral mush ("great results", "real value"). Use the specific from the source, or leave a fill-in marker for the owner. Never invent one.

## Before and after
Before: "In today's fast-paced home services landscape, our dedicated team delivers seamless, world-class solutions that empower homeowners to navigate their plumbing needs with confidence."

After: "Plumbing problems are stressful. We make them simple. Tell us what's leaking and we'll be there the same day. No jargon, no runaround."
