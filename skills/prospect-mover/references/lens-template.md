# Vertical lens: template

Copy this file, rename it for your vertical (for example `lens-dental.md`), and fill it in. Keep it to what is true for your buyers and your rules. `lens-example.md` shows a finished one.

Load a lens only for work in its own vertical. Never let it leak into another.

## The buyer

- Who exactly buys, and what does a normal week look like for them?
- What job are they hiring this for?
- What do they do instead today?
