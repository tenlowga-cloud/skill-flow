# Move Score

Ten checks, each 0 to 10. Score the original first, then the rebuild. Give one line of evidence per check. A 9 means a tired prospect on a phone gets it, believes it and acts. The daylight test (SKILL.md) is pass/fail and sits above every score.

| # | Check | 10 looks like | 3 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Stop** | The first line is about the reader, specific, and earns the second line. | Opens with the sender, a greeting, "I hope this finds you well", or a vague claim. |
| 2 | **One point** | A stranger can say what it's about in 5 seconds. | Three ideas compete. You have to read twice. |
| 3 | **Felt gap** | The reader feels where they are, where they could be, and the cost of standing still, in their own terms. | Features listed. No problem named, or the problem is the sender's ("we need more clients"). |
| 4 | **You focus** | "You" outnumbers "we/I/our" about 2 to 1 or better. The reader is the hero, the sender is the guide. | The piece is a biography of the company. |
| 5 | **Proof** | Specific, checkable, close to the claim: a number with a source, a named result, a credential that matters to *this* buyer. | "Best in class", "trusted by many", no evidence, or proof buried at the bottom. |
| 6 | **Ease** | The path is 3 steps or fewer, the risk is removed or named, the top objection is answered before it's asked. | The reader has to figure out what happens next, or fears a trap. |
| 7 | **Close path** | One clear ask that is the right next rung toward the stated close, sized to the trust earned so far, in plain words, with an honest reason to act now. Every beat sets it up. | No ask, two or more asks, an ask that doesn't lead toward the close, or a huge ask from a cold reader ("buy now" on a first touch). |
| 8 | **Vibe** | Warm first, competent second. Calm confidence. Sounds like a specific trusted person. | Needy, pushy, robotic, salesy, or so cautious it says nothing. |
| 9 | **Visual pull** (skip for plain text and spoken) | Clear hierarchy, one accent color on the one action, generous white space, readable at phone width, premium feel. | Clutter, many colors fighting, tiny type, the button doesn't stand out. |
| 10 | **Fluency** | Short common words, short sentences with varied rhythm, scannable, reads aloud clean. Grade 5 to 7 for consumers. | Jargon, long sentences, walls of text, acronyms. |

Spoken pieces swap check 9 for **Pace**: pauses, tone shifts, a question the prospect answers within the first 20 seconds, no monologue longer than 30 seconds.

## Reading the score

- **Lowest check first.** The weakest link is usually the leak. Fix it before polishing anything else.
- **8 on everything is not done.** It means the piece is fine. Fine doesn't move people.
- **A 10 that fails the daylight test is a 0.**
- **Score evidence, not hope.** If you can't point to the line that earns the score, it doesn't earn it.
