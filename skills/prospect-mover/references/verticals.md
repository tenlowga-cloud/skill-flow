# Verticals: read the market before you move it

The systems in this skill work for any product, service, cause or hire. What changes by vertical is the buyer, what they fear, what they'd do instead, what counts as proof and what the next step looks like. Each new vertical starts empty. Don't carry one industry's buyer, vocabulary, metaphors, look or compliance assumptions into another. Regulated work loads `verticals.md#regulated-work`. An owner's own deep vertical lens, if they have one, loads only for that vertical (`lens-example.md` shows the shape, `lens-template.md` is the blank).

## The adapter (fill for every piece)

Answer these from the project's own truth first (site, reviews, call notes, the offer, past campaigns). Infer the rest, label it `inferred`, and keep going.

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Who is the one buyer?** Role, life stage, what a normal Tuesday looks like for them | Copy for "everyone" moves no one |
| **What job are they hiring this for?** The outcome, not the product (a quarter-inch hole, not the drill) | The headline sells the job |
| **What do they do instead today?** Status quo, a competitor, a workaround, nothing | The real competitor is usually "keep doing what I do" |
| **What does staying put cost them?** Money, time, risk, status, stress, missed upside | That's the honest gap |
| **What do they fear about buying?** Wasting money, looking dumb, a pushy seller, hidden costs, effort, regret | The ease beat removes this |
| **What proof does this buyer trust?** Reviews, a demo, case studies, credentials, a guarantee, a referral, a sample | Proof that impresses sellers often means nothing to buyers |
| **What triggers a purchase?** A season, a life event, a deadline, a breakdown, a new hire, a budget cycle | The honest reason to act now |
| **What's the smallest next step?** Reply, a call, a sample, a trial, a visit, a quote, a cart | Size the ask to the trust earned |
| **What words do they use?** Pull from reviews, forms, calls, forums, objections | Their words are the best copy there is |
| **What rules apply?** Regulated claims, platform ad rules, consent for texts and calls, privacy | Gates before persuasion |

## Quick lenses

Starting points, not facts about any particular business. Confirm against the project.

| Vertical | Status quo to beat | Proof they trust | Honest triggers | Smallest step | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Local trades and home services** (roofing, HVAC, plumbing, landscaping) | Wait until it breaks, or ask a neighbor | Reviews count and recency, photos of real jobs, license, warranty | Storms, seasons, a failure, selling the house | Free inspection, photo quote by text | Never promise response times or prices you can't keep |
| **B2B services and agencies** | In-house, the current vendor, "next quarter" | Named results for similar companies, a specific teardown, referrals | Missed targets, a new leader, budget planning, a failed launch | 15-minute call with a specific insight | Talk outcomes and numbers, not your process |
| **SaaS and software** | Spreadsheets, the incumbent tool, doing nothing | A live demo, a free trial, integrations, peer logos (real ones) | Team growth, a breaking workflow, renewal of the current tool | Trial, demo, a template | Setup effort is the hidden objection. Show time to first win |
| **E-commerce and DTC** | The brand they already buy, Amazon, not buying | Reviews with photos, UGC, returns policy, a clear guarantee | Gifting seasons, a new need, running out | Add to cart, a sample size, a quiz | Fake countdowns and fake "only 3 left" are banned here too |
| **Health, wellness and fitness** | Doing nothing, a free app, a past failed attempt | Real before/after with consent, credentials, a trial class | New year, an event, a doctor's nudge, a milestone birthday | First session, a free class, a call | Health claims are regulated. No promised results |
| **Coaching, courses and education** | YouTube, figuring it out alone, "when I have time" | Student outcomes, a free lesson, the teacher's own results | A career change, a goal date, a cohort start | Free lesson, a webinar, an application | Income claims need substantiation. No guaranteed outcomes |
| **Real estate** | Waiting for the market, a friend-of-a-friend agent, FSBO | Recent local sales, a pricing analysis, reviews | Life events (baby, job, divorce, empty nest), rate changes | A home value estimate, a short call | Fair housing rules on targeting and wording |
| **Restaurants, hospitality and events** | The usual place, cooking at home | Photos, reviews, a menu, a friend's visit | Weekends, holidays, celebrations, the weather | Reserve, order, claim a first-visit offer | Keep the offer simple and the button obvious |
| **Recruiting and hiring** (pitching candidates or partners) | Their current job or org | People like them who made the move, real comp ranges | Bad reviews, a reorg, a missed promotion, slow pay | A 15-minute confidential chat | Non-solicit agreements and no disparaging competitors |
| **Nonprofits and fundraising** | Giving elsewhere or not at all | One named person helped, transparent numbers, matching gifts | Year end, a crisis, a match deadline (real) | A small first gift, a monthly default | Identifiable-victim stories need consent and dignity |
| **Insurance and financial** | "I'll get to it", the policy they set up years ago | Precise numbers for their situation, credentials, reviews | Birthdays (rate changes), new house, new baby, renewal, retirement | A quick review, a quote | Load `verticals.md#regulated-work`. A worked vertical lens is in `lens-example.md` |

## How to use it

1. Fill the adapter in three to ten lines. It goes in your head or the change log, not in the piece.
2. Pick the frame by awareness level (`structure.md#awareness`) and the format playbook (`formats.md`).
3. Rewrite every example and metaphor in the piece in the buyer's world. A roofer's homeowner gets weather and shingles, a SaaS buyer gets hours saved and fewer tabs.
4. Run the layers and the gates as usual.

## Regulated work

Load for any piece in a regulated vertical. Persuasion still applies, but truth and fairness are the law here, not just good manners. Rules vary by country, state, product and channel. This is a checklist, not legal advice: when a rule matters to the piece, check the current source or the business's compliance contact, and a human makes the final call.

### Every vertical

- **Substantiate every objective claim.** Results, savings, speed, "#1", "best" and before/after claims need evidence on file before they run.
- **Testimonials and reviews** must be real, from real customers, with typical results or a clear disclosure when they are not. No fake, bought or gated reviews.
- **Consent for texts and calls**, unsubscribe and a physical address for email, and honest subject lines.
- **Platform rules.** Credit, housing, employment, insurance, health and political ads often fall in special categories with targeting limits.
- **Privacy.** Collect only what you need, and say what you will do with it.

### By vertical

- **Health, wellness, supplements:** no claims to treat, cure or prevent disease without approval. Results claims need evidence, and before/after photos need consent and typical-results context.
- **Income, coaching, business opportunities:** earnings claims need substantiation and typical-results disclosure. No guaranteed income.
- **Real estate and lending:** fair housing rules on targeting and wording. Lending ads need required disclosures when they name a rate.
- **Employment and recruiting:** no discriminatory targeting or wording. Respect non-solicit agreements. Don't disparage the candidate's current employer.
- **Legal and professional services:** licensing board advertising rules. No guaranteed outcomes.
- **Insurance, annuities and financial products:** the strictest of these. `lens-example.md` is a worked example of a full vertical lens built for exactly this case.

### Persuasion that stays safe in regulated work

- The cost of the gap, in true numbers for the reader's situation.
- Real triggers only: a renewal, a rate change date, a life event, an enrollment window.
- Fear stays factual. Older and grieving readers get extra care: clear, slow, easy to involve family.
- A review or check offered as a real service, with what happens next stated plainly.
